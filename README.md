# AirMush-Smart-Air-Purification-System
Conceptual design of a modular air purification device, using cyclotronic motion as driving mechanism and bio-filters as natural purifiers.

## Overview

This project was developed as part of the **IBEC Engineering Challenge 2025**, where multidisciplinary student teams were tasked with proposing an innovative engineering solution to a real-world environmental problem within a single day.

Our challenge focused on the deteriorating air quality in Turin, a city that frequently experiences elevated levels of atmospheric pollution due to industrial emissions, traffic, and unfavorable geographic conditions. The objective was to develop a conceptual air purification device capable of contributing to cleaner urban air while considering practicality, sustainability, and manufacturability.

As part of the team, I was responsible for the CAD design and mechanical development of the proposed device. The concept was modeled in SolidWorks, allowing the team to visualize the design, communicate the proposed solution, and present a functional engineering concept during the final evaluation. Furthermore, an App design was adapted, in order to motivate citizens and track and connect devices around the city.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a conceptual solution to improve urban air quality.
- Design a mechanically feasible and modular air purification system.
- Produce a complete 3D CAD model for presentation.
- Communicate the engineering concept through technical documentation.
- Complete the project within the one-day competition timeframe.
  
## Key Features of the Project

- Modular construction for easy maintenance
- Compact and scalable architecture
- Sustainable and green-energy oriented
